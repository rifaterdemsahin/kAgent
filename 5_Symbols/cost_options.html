Key Takeaways for This PoC

Fly.io Nested k3s — Cheapest Managed Experiment

Gives full control of k3s at micro-VM pricing without control-plane overhead. In exchange, you pay in engineering effort to manage state, backups, and Firecracker cold starts — exactly the DNS-resolver and TLS-less-BusyBox issues this project's own deployment timeline hit and fixed.

Civo / Managed Light K8s — Best Developer-Experience PoC

Costs roughly the same as Fly.io (~$22/mo) but gives native, multi-node Kubernetes out-of-the-box — no container-in-VM hacks, no control-plane fee, and no per-project DNS/TLS gotchas to debug.

GKE / EKS / AKS — Overkill for a PoC

Hyperscalers charge a fixed ~$73/month control-plane fee regardless of cluster size, making a tiny single-node PoC disproportionately expensive compared to either alternative above.

Estimates only — actual pricing depends on region, provider tier, and current rate cards. This project's real Fly.io deployment (SPEC-018) also runs a 3-hour idle auto-stop so compute cost accrues only while actually in use, which the flat monthly figures above don't reflect.
