What happens when you invoke k8s-agent

Live test confirmed on both deployments 2026-09-10: sent a real message/send JSON-RPC call asking "What is a Kubernetes Pod, in one sentence?" (minikube) and "what is a Kubernetes Deployment?" (Fly.io). Both returned state: completed with a correct, on-topic answer generated by deepseek-chat.

kubectl port-forward / Fly proxy
→
kagent-controller (A2A JSON-RPC)
→
k8s-agent pod (ADK engine)
→
api.deepseek.com/chat/completions
200 — answer returned

Getting here took a real DeepSeek account balance (initially 402 Insufficient Balance — see R-S10 in risks.md, resolved once credits were added) and, on Fly.io specifically, two build-time fixes and a DNS fix — full story in deployment_timeline.md and deepseek.md.
